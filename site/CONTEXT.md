## Current implementation

- Six VDS9 precompiled semantic pages: home, services, legal notice, privacy, cookies and accessibility.
- Search manifests, sitemap, robots, `llms.txt` and per-page knowledge surfaces are generated by the engine.
- The hero is a CSS-only film: eight cross-fading frames / 45 s on desktop and five vertical frames / 35 s on mobile, with the original site hero as the first and last frame, slow Ken Burns movement and a reduced-motion fallback.
- Mobile is a dedicated composition: 3×2 compact programme/service grids, accordion disclosure for long editorial and legal content, a full-screen fade menu with animated links and contact footer, and a back-to-top control.
- Styles are minified and inlined at build time; images ship as WebP with JPEG fallback and responsive variants.
- The design is mobile-first and uses self-hosted Open Sans and Cormorant Garamond, local photography, official WhatsApp brand glyphs and no third-party runtime.
- Source material and downloaded originals are archived under the workspace `source/` directory; the generated release lives in `site/dist/`.
- Build: `node src/build.mjs` from `site/`. Preview: `python -m http.server 8123 --directory site/dist`. Engine dependency: `../../engine/VDS-ENGINE-9`.
