## Development environment

Primary workflow: **VS Code + Kilo Code + DeepSeek API**. The repository instructions are model-independent; switching model/provider must not change architectural or quality requirements.

## Source of truth

Project decisions belong in version-controlled source and documentation. Chat history is not authoritative. When architecture or workflow changes, update the relevant project document in the same change.

## Current implementation

- Six VDS9 precompiled semantic pages: home, services, legal notice, privacy, cookies and accessibility.
- Search manifests, sitemap, robots, `llms.txt` and per-page knowledge surfaces are generated by the engine.
- The hero is a 45-second CSS-only film: eight cross-fading frames, the original site hero as the first and last frame, art-directed desktop and mobile sources, slow Ken Burns movement and a reduced-motion fallback.
- The design is mobile-first and uses self-hosted Open Sans and Cormorant Garamond, local photography, official WhatsApp brand glyphs and no third-party runtime.
- Source material and downloaded originals are archived under the workspace `source/` directory; the generated release lives in `site/dist/`.
- Build: `node src/build.mjs` from `site/`. Preview: `python -m http.server 8123 --directory site/dist`. Engine dependency: `../../engine/VDS-ENGINE-9`.
